A $5 deposit that comes back the moment you walk in.

Shops lose tables and appointments to no-shows, and a card deposit doesn't pay for itself: on $5, a typical 2.9% + 30¢ card fee takes about 9%, and a card refund takes 5–10 business days (Stripe pricing, refunds FAQ). ShowUp holds the deposit in USDC on Arc instead. Check in, and it is back in your wallet before you sit down. Don't, and the shop can claim it after your slot.

  1. Book. Lock the shop's deposit for a time slot. Free cancellation until the shop's cutoff.
  2. Walk in. The shop shows a QR — a check-in pass signed by its key, valid for three minutes. Submitting it refunds the deposit to the wallet that booked (the shop's tablet can submit it, so the guest needs no gas).
  3. Or don't. Only after the slot plus the shop's grace period (at least 5 minutes) can the shop claim it, and only to its payout address.

Why Arc

On Arc, USDC is the gas. The deposit and the fee come out of one dollar balance: a guest needs no second token to book, and a shop needs no ETH to run. Each action costs about a cent, so a $5 deposit is worth protecting. Finality is sub-second, so the refund is final before the guest reaches the table. Measured fees and confirmation times are in the README.

Trust model

The shop attests that you came by signing your pass. A shop could withhold the pass and claim anyway, so every shop's record is public and counted on chain: its age, how many different guests booked, how many checked in and how many deposits it claimed as no-shows. That claim rate is a signal, not proof — check it before you book. A shop can't book itself, and terms can't change after registration. No admin key exists; the deployer cannot touch deposits. If a shop doesn't claim within 30 days after the slot, or disappears, the guest can take a still-held deposit back.

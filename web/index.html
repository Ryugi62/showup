A $5 deposit that comes back the moment you walk in.

Shops lose tables and appointments to no-shows, but a card deposit costs more in fees and chargebacks than it protects. ShowUp holds a small USDC deposit on Arc. Show up, and the shop's check-in pass refunds it at the door in under a second. Don't, and the shop can claim it after your slot. Nobody can take it early — there is no admin key.

  1. Book. The customer locks the shop's deposit (e.g. 5 USDC) for a time slot. Free cancellation until the shop's cutoff.
  2. Walk in. The shop shows a one-time QR (an EIP-712 pass signed by its check-in key). Scanning it refunds the deposit to the customer's wallet.
  3. Or don't. After the slot plus a grace period, only the shop can claim the deposit, only to its payout address.

Why Arc

Gas is paid in USDC and costs about a cent, priced in dollars — so a $5 deposit is worth protecting. Finality is sub-second, so the refund settles before the customer reaches the table. On a $5 card deposit, a typical online card rate of 2.9% + 30¢ takes about 45¢ (9%), and a card refund takes 5–10 business days to reach the customer (Stripe pricing, Stripe refunds FAQ).

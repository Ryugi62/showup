Check-in key

Keep the owner wallet off the counter: a separate check-in key on the shop's tablet signs passes without a wallet prompt. It can only authorize check-ins (which refund the guest who booked) — never claim, pause, change settings or withdraw. It is stored in this browser only — create it in the browser the tablet will use at the counter. If site data is cleared, create a new one here. To submit check-ins without any wallet, send the key about $1 of USDC for fees (keep it under $5; "Sweep" moves it to your payout address).

Register a new shop

Deposit, cancel window and grace are fixed once registered, so they never change under an existing booking. Payout address and check-in key start as the connected wallet; change them under Shop settings. Claim no-shows within 30 days of the slot — after that the guest can take the deposit back.
